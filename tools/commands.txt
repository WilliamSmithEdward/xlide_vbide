SWITCH BACK TO THE DEV BUILD
============================

1. Close Excel.

2. Paste this into PowerShell:

powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File "F:\GitHub\xlide\xlide_vbide\tools\Restore-DevRegistration.ps1"

3. Open Excel and press Alt+F11.


That one command does everything: it removes the installed product if it is there, points the
editor back at your dev build, and checks that it worked before it says it worked.

It works whether or not the installer was ever run.
